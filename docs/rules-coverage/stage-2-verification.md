# Stage 2 — selection ownership, modifier and QA verification

Reviewed 2026-11-13. Rule interpretations below were checked directly on the permitted public `tow.whfb.app` rule pages by the lead and independent rules reviewer. This is a narrow verification of the assertions listed, not a complete FAQ/errata audit or confirmation of every item interaction.

## Rules evidence and obligations

| Source | Verified assertion and implemented boundary |
| --- | --- |
| [Charmed Shield](https://tow.whfb.app/magic-item/charmed-shield) | Magic Armour; shield; **bearer** has a 5+ Ward against **one wound once per game**. After that use it is an ordinary shield. Resolver requires consistent item URL and name. Defence shows bearer-specific availability and battle session records that use; Effects reports the same item's readiness. No automatic save roll. |
| [Seed of Rebirth](https://tow.whfb.app/magic-item/seed-of-rebirth) | Enchanted item granting **Regeneration (5+) to its bearer**. Resolves on the individual character/champion only; no regiment-wide Regeneration. No regeneration-roll engine. |
| [The Loremaster's Cloak](https://tow.whfb.app/magic-item/the-loremasters-cloak) | The bearer and unit they joined receive a **4+ Ward against Magic Missile wounds**. Recorded joined-character assignments are surfaced conditionally; legality is not inferred. |

## Regression evidence and compatibility checks

The Stage 1 **Case A** fixture assigns an imported Charmed Shield name to Seed of Rebirth's URL; the original Defence code granted the Ward while Effects rejected it. The **Case B** fixture nests correctly sourced Charmed Shield under Equipment; original Defence missed it while Effects found it. Tests were observed failing against the pre-fix engines before implementing the shared resolver; tests now assert both engines' decisions, owners, provenance, and missing/conflicting source exclusions. Additional negative cases cover model group, champion, character, repeated unit instances, removal/replacement, published defaults and spent/restored one-use state. The original selection and army text are left unmodified by the resolver.

For save-state compatibility, `schemaVersion: 1` and the existing `usedCharmedShields` / `itemStates` structures remain. A stale or source-conflicting shield use flag cannot activate an unverified item. Saved use flags remain opaque until source verification and are carried through edits during reference loading; the initial IndexedDB read precedes writes, preventing premature overwrite. Normal and Guided Play continue to share `battle-session.js`; the UI passes the same source records to Defence as Effects. The service-worker precache includes the new resolver, source profile snapshot, and sample fixtures with a bumped cache name. The modifier helper has no automatic callers and no stored state.

## Review boundaries and remaining Stage 3 dependencies

- The resolver uses structured imported command/role qualifiers; it does not guess faction-specific champion names. Individual rank-and-file item bearers and component-only rider/mount/crew references remain unresolved without explicit model identification. Legal membership and joined-character assignments are separate facts; a recorded assignment is not proof of legality.
- Published starting defaults require a matching source-extracted unit record. Options from upgrade catalogues are not automatically selected. Source-extracted replacement instructions are considered only when the selected option and source-linked identities agree. More complicated replacement grammars and source contradictions require manual review.
- Target-specific save contexts, spell phase expiry, mount/chariot split profiles, Ward-versus-Regeneration ordering, complete stacking, one-use champion flags and all unreviewed source mechanics remain outside Stage 2.
- The lead review examined resolver identity, engine parity, preserved state schema, negative cases and service-worker references. Four specialist reviews were completed: **Euclid** checked Charmed Shield, Seed of Rebirth, Loremaster's Cloak, Razor Standard, mundane armour/shields and Armour Bane against the original source pages and the relevant Characters, Universal Special Rules and Magic Items FAQs; **Erdos** reviewed name/URL consistency, owner scope and negative cases, leading to six additional exclusions; **Mill** reviewed UI, offline, saved state and asynchronous source loading, leading to extra precache entries and a deferred-state safeguard; **Nietzsche** reviewed coverage evidence and prevented whole-rule status promotions. The reviews support these scoped obligations only; source FAQ/errata closure and complete browser-device testing remain open.
- The full local Windows Node suite passes **410/410** tests, including resolver, modifier, Defence/Effects parity, one-use persistence, importer and offline cache checks. Three baseline fixture failures were corrected by making the import-guard comparisons and Wood Elf equipment insertion independent of Windows `CRLF` versus Unix `LF` newlines. The Stage 2 app now waits for the saved session read before applying any player edit, preserving unrelated stored assignments, spell activity and personal names. `node scripts/build-rules-coverage.mjs --check`, `node --check docs/app.js` and `git diff --check` also pass. Browser/mobile/offline workflow results must be checked separately on the PR.

**Suggested Stage 3:** verified joined-character legality and split-profile model/component identity, source-extracted role mapping through the importer, one-use flags per eligible model, then scoped phase/expiry enforcement. Retain the existing shared resolver and modifier representation without adding a broad save-roll or combat engine prematurely.
