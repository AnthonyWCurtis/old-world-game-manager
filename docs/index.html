Important rules and profile limitations
   
Character assignments are recorded, not rules-verified. Rules on joining units, Fly and Ethereal can restrict joins. The game must not infer equipment, buffs or legality from membership alone.

   
Personal unit names and wizard spell selections save separately on this device and never modify the original import. Profiles are published base characteristics. Selected equipment, spells and upgrades do not automatically alter them; this is not a legality or points calculator.

   
Defence summaries distinguish equipment-only armour and source-backed conditional Ward and Regeneration effects. Magic Missile-only saves are not universal. Source-reviewed banners and magic items show their exact scope and any conditions; other selections are not automatically assigned effects. One-use items need manual confirmation. A Ward against Magic Missiles is not a universal Ward; Charmed Shield is a once-per-game item. Enemy AP, targeting, automatic casting and legal joins are not assumed.
